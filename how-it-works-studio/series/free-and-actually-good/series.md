## How this series should sound

- She is the friend who already read the manual, not the teacher who assigns it.
- One idea per video. The caveat is part of the idea, not a disclaimer at the end.
- No scare framing, no 'they don't want you to know'. The system is boring and that is the relief.

## Shared visual motifs

Market-stall isometrics: crates, price tags, a till. Playful, a bit bazaar.

Keep the motifs consistent inside a series and different between series — a viewer should know
which series they are watching before she speaks.
